# Seed Vault manual test (do it yourself, before and after each change)

None of this goes through the computer or the AI. Photograph nothing, and never type your words anywhere but in the Seed Vault.

1. Open Seed Vault Wallet: it asks for your fingerprint, then your password if you choose "use password". Both must work.
2. Check that your account or accounts show the right public address (compare the first 4 and last 4 characters with a source you trust, for example an explorer where you already received funds).
3. Signing: connect to a trusted dApp that offers "Sign in with Solana" or "sign a message". The Seed Vault window must appear and ask for your fingerprint. Signing a message costs nothing and moves no funds.
4. Scan an address QR code from the wallet (this breaks if Play Services is disabled).
5. Settings > Seed Vault (or seed-vault > seed vault in the launcher): the app opens.

## Check you have an offline backup, without revealing it
- Take out your paper or metal backup, alone, with no phone or camera around.
- In the Seed Vault, open the recovery phrase (it asks for fingerprint and password) and compare word by word, number by number, with your backup. Then close it.
- If a single word differs, or if you have no backup: write it now, by hand, and keep it away from the phone.
- Never type it into another app, website or device, and never give it to anyone, AI included.
